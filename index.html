About Moon South Pole Mission Planner

This interactive tool models solar illumination profiles, direct Earth radio line-of-sight availability, and geological analog matching for candidate landing sites across the lunar South Pole.

Key Scientific Context

The lunar South Pole is of intense scientific interest due to Permanently Shadowed Regions (PSRs) that trap water ice and volatiles, alongside nearby elevated ridges receiving long-duration solar illumination.
